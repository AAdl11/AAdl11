## 🌱 Miya · Seeds of Kindness · 米芽 · 種子的善意

**[▶ Play](https://aadl11.github.io/kindness-seeds-/)** · **[Repo](https://github.com/AAdl11/kindness-seeds-)** · **[DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20818912](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20818912)** · **[Backstage AI architecture](https://github.com/AAdl11/kindness-seeds-/blob/main/docs/AI_ARCHITECTURE.md)**

*Capstone project for the Kaggle × Google 5-Day AI Agents Intensive, 2026.*

A trilingual community game in five levels, built around the **Three Blessings** — a home, a
neighborhood, a school — the framework behind twenty years of care in Bayview–Hunters Point.
Free, offline, playable in any browser. No scores, no ranking, no losing.

**Backstage runs a multi-agent content pipeline.** A Strategy Planner draws the map. A
Coordinator dispatches three workers: a **Guide** agent for voice, a **Content** agent for
level configs, a **Safety** agent to review and hold. Then a **human review gate** — the one
path into `data/`. The player-facing runtime calls no AI at all.

The Guide's five rules rest on two independent bodies of research: Carol Dweck's growth
mindset and Rudolf Dreikurs' Adlerian positive discipline. Two traditions that never talked
to each other, arriving at the same place. One of the rules: when a child gets something
wrong, the first thing they hear is *"that's a tricky one."*

三語社區遊戲，五關對應**三福**。後台是一套多代理內容管線，人審是唯一寫入權限；
玩家端 runtime 完全不呼叫 AI。免費、離線、任何瀏覽器都能玩。

## 🧩 AIAO · AI Agent Optimization

*「讓 AI 把對的人，帶到你面前」*

A consulting method for **mission-driven organizations working with what they have**: shaping
what an organization already is into a form AI agents can read and are glad to recommend —
the work that matters once discovery moves from keywords to agent recommendation.
Vendor-neutral, bilingual, sized for small charities. Miya and Journey of Kindness are its
worked examples.

幫使命導向、資源有限的組織，把自身價值整理成 AI 代理讀得懂、樂於推薦的形式。

## How I work · 幾個原則

- **Data-driven, engine-frozen** — content lives in `data/`, the engine stays put. Adding a lesson means adding data. 內容與引擎分離，加一課＝加一份資料。
- **Every AI line passes a human before it ships.** 每一句 AI 產出，都由人讀過才上線。
- **Offline, no install, no login** — plain HTML/CSS/JS, so it runs on whatever the classroom already has. 純靜態、免安裝、免登入、可離線。
- **Trilingual from the first draft** — EN · 中文 · Español. 三語同步，不是事後翻譯。

## Where the name comes from · 米芽的由來

**Miya 米芽 means a rice sprout.**

In 2006, at John Muir Elementary in Hunters Point, **Sister Roxanne Buchwitz 黃淑雲師姊** met a
child who had come to school with nothing for breakfast — not even a handful of raw rice.
She left an executive career for that community. **A single handful of rice grew into twenty
years of care**: food shares, blankets, tutoring, free clinics, the families at the RV Park.
The **Three Blessings** — home, neighborhood, school — is the framework she built there. The
game carries her structure, and it carries her rice.

I joined Tzu Chi in San Francisco in 2009 and have volunteered across the city since — food
distribution, classrooms, tutoring, Sundays at **Tzu Chi Academy**.

In one of those classrooms, an after-school math program ran on software that read each
child and met them where they stood: harder when they were ready, gentler when they needed
it. **That was adaptive learning. I stood beside an algorithm doing what I now build, more
than a decade before I had a name for it.**

At 58 I went back to school for the name — and for how to build it myself. I completed the
**AI Certification Program at Las Positas College**: 21 units, Python and C++, and Russell &
Norvig's *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* twice through — CS4 for search, logic
and planning; CS5 for learning. **What I hold is one certificate, two projects that run, and
a textbook I read the way you read a book you intend to use.**

這個遊戲承接她的架構，也承接她那一把米。

2009 年我加入慈濟，在舊金山做社區服務至今——食物發放、進教室、課後輔導，

五十八歲，我回學校去學那個名字，以及怎麼自己把它做出來。
我完成的是 **Las Positas College 的 AI Certification Program**：21 學分，Python 和 C++，

**我手上有的是一張證書、兩個跑得起來的專案，以及一本用「準備拿它來做事」的方式讀過的書。**
