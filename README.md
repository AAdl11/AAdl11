## How I got here · 怎麼走到這裡的

I have volunteered with Tzu Chi across San Francisco since 2009, and was certified as a
commissioner in July 2018.

Monday mornings, at **Gordon J. Lau Elementary** in Chinatown, we handed groceries to
parents dropping their children off — families living in SRO rooms, most of them
low-income, many newly arrived. That ran until the pandemic stopped it.

Then the work concentrated in **Bayview–Hunters Point**: five elementary schools and one
high school that the city had largely stopped looking at. We sat in classrooms because
there weren't enough teachers to go around. I spent most of my hours at **Malcolm X** — after-school
tutoring, and mornings as a teaching assistant in the Chinese-language class. I was
managing a bar at the time, so I assisted rather than led; leading means lesson planning,
and I did not have those hours to give.

Sundays I helped at **Tzu Chi Academy**.

And in the after-school math program we ran with the Red Cross, Roxanne's husband Matt — then
an executive at Genentech — taught with a piece of software that watched each child and
adjusted the difficulty to them: harder questions when they were ready, easier when they
weren't.

**That was adaptive learning. I was standing next to an algorithm doing exactly what I now
build, more than a decade before I knew what to call it.**

I went back to school at 58 to learn what to call it — and how to build it myself.

To be exact about what that was: I completed the **AI Certification Program at Las Positas
College** — 21 units, Python and C++, and Russell & Norvig's *Artificial Intelligence: A
Modern Approach* twice over. CS4 for search, logic and planning; CS5 for learning. I am not
a computer science major and I do not hold a CS degree. What I have is one certificate, two
shipped projects, and a book I have read the way you read a book you intend to use.

## 這一段的中文

2009 年加入慈濟，在舊金山做社區服務，2018 年 7 月受證。

每週一早上，在中國城的**劉貴明小學 Gordon J. Lau Elementary**，我們和食物銀行合作，
在家長送孩子上學的時候把食物交到他們手上——那些家庭多半住在散房，低收入，
不少是剛從大陸移民過來的。這個活動一直做到疫情才停。

之後心力集中到 **Bayview–Hunters Point**：五所小學和一所高中，
一群幾乎被城市遺忘的學校。師資不夠，我們就進教室協助維持課堂。
我待最久的是 **Malcolm X**——課後輔導，早上在中文班當助教。
那時候我還在做酒吧管理，所以是助教不是主教；主教要備課，那個時間我拿不出來。

週日在**慈濟人文學校 Tzu Chi Academy** 幫忙。

而我們跟紅十字會合作的課後數學班，是 Roxanne 的先生 Matt 帶的——他當時是 Genentech 的高管。

## Working principles · 幾個原則

- **Data-driven, engine-frozen** — content lives in `data/`, the engine doesn't move. Adding a lesson means adding data, not rewriting code. 內容與引擎分離，加一課＝加一份資料。
- **No unreviewed AI output reaches a learner** — every AI-generated line passes a human review gate before it ships. 未經人審的 AI 產出，絕不進入學習者畫面。
- **Offline, no install, no login** — plain HTML/CSS/JS. It has to run on whatever device the classroom actually has. 純靜態、免安裝、免登入、可離線。
- **Trilingual by default** — EN · 中文 · Español. 三語同步，不是事後翻譯。

## Projects · 專案

### 🌱 Miya · Seeds of Kindness · 種子的善意
**[▶ Play](https://aadl11.github.io/kindness-seeds-/)** · **[Repo](https://github.com/AAdl11/kindness-seeds-)** · **[DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20818912](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20818912)** · **[Backstage AI architecture](https://github.com/AAdl11/kindness-seeds-/blob/main/docs/AI_ARCHITECTURE.md)**

*Submitted as the capstone project for the Kaggle × Google 5-Day AI Agents Intensive, 2026.*

A trilingual community game in five levels — home, neighborhood, school — drawn from the
work in Hunters Point that began in 2006 with one handful of rice at John Muir Elementary.
No scores, no ranking, no losing.

Behind it runs a **multi-agent content pipeline**: a Strategy Planner sets the map, a
Coordinator dispatches a Guide agent (voice), a Content agent (level configs) and a Safety
agent (review and block) — and a **human review gate** is the only thing that can write into
`data/`. The player-facing runtime calls no AI at all.

The Guide's five rules sit on two independent bodies of research — Carol Dweck's growth
mindset and Rudolf Dreikurs' Adlerian positive discipline. One of them: when a child gets
something wrong, the first response is never "wrong." It is "that's a tricky one."

三語社區遊戲，五關對應幸福家園／社區／校園。後台是一套多代理內容管線，
人審是唯一寫入權限；玩家端 runtime 完全不呼叫 AI。

### 🧭 Journey of Kindness · 善的旅程
**[▶ Play](https://aadl11.github.io/journey-of-kindness/)** · **[Repo](https://github.com/AAdl11/journey-of-kindness)** · **[Paper: *When Algorithms Remember What We Forget*](https://github.com/AAdl11/journey-of-kindness/blob/main/docs/Hsu_Final_Complete.pdf)**

Seven levels, seven classical AI algorithms, each one taught through a real community
problem — A\* search to route meals to elders, MDP to plan under uncertainty, alpha-beta
pruning to face an ethical dilemma. Between levels, an *Ataraxy Portico* (靜心之門) for a
breath and a Jing Si aphorism.

Capstone for CS4 · Introduction to AI, Las Positas College.

七關七個古典演算法，每一關都是一個真實的社區問題。關與關之間設「靜心之門」。

### 🧩 AIAO · AI Agent Optimization
*"讓 AI 把對的人，帶到你面前"*

A consulting method for **mission-driven, resource-limited organizations**: shaping what an
organization already is into a form AI agents can read and are willing to recommend — the
work that matters once discovery shifts from keywords to agent recommendation.
Vendor-neutral, bilingual, built for small charities. Miya and Journey of Kindness are its
worked examples.

幫使命導向、資源有限的組織，把自身價值整理成 AI 代理讀得懂、樂於推薦的形式。
